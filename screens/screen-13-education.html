The Added Sugar Illusion

Manufacturers often use 4 different types of sugar (maltodextrin, concentrated fruit purée, invert sugar, dextrose) so that none appear as the #1 ingredient.

warning
Serving Size Tricks

A cookie pack may list “30 Calories”, but define a serving size as 1/3 of a single cookie.

Deciphering INS Codes

INS 100-199 are colors, 200-299 are preservatives, 300-399 are antioxidants, and 400-499 are stabilizers.
